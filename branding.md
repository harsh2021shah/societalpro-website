## SocietalPro: Brand Guide (v2, Transparency Movement)

### 1. Brand belief

**Manifesto** *(for the About page, pitch and launch post)*

> A country is not its government. A country is its people, and people are shaped by the places they learn to live together.
>
>
> We learn honesty at home. The next place we practice it is our society: who collects the money, where it goes, how decisions get made.
>
> When a society is open, people get used to asking questions and getting answers. They get used to doing the right thing when no one is forcing them to.
>
> Habits travel. A person who expects transparency from their society will one day expect it everywhere.
>
> SocietalPro is a private company with a simple belief: **honest societies build an honest country.** We start with yours.
>

**Mission:** To help every housing society run in the open, so transparency becomes an everyday habit.

**Vision:** A country where people grew up expecting openness, because they lived it in their own society first.

**Positioning statement**

> For committees and residents who want their society run honestly, SocietalPro is the society management platform that puts every payment, decision and action on record, so nothing is hidden and trust doesn’t depend on any one person.
>

**Category:** Society management platform (AI-assisted) with a civic purpose.

### 4. Audience

| Audience | Why they care | What they feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Honest Secretary / Treasurer** (primary buyer) | Tired of suspicion, Excel and WhatsApp chaos; wants proof of honest work | “I want to be trusted, and I want less to do.” |
| **Residents** (daily users) | Want to know where the money goes, pay easily, be heard in their language | “I just want clarity.” |
| **Young and civic-minded residents** (champions) | Want to be part of change, not just complain | “This is how I make my society better.” |
| **Later:** builders, property managers | Run many societies with consistent standards | Scale and reputation |

**The brand’s three-step habit loop (central idea)**

**See** (where the money goes) → **Ask** (raise requests, vote, question) → **Decide** (together, on record).

This is the “habit of transparency” in product form and works as a repeating visual and content framework.

### 5. Messaging pillars

1. **Honesty starts at home.** Your society is where honesty becomes a habit.
2. **Nothing hidden.** Payments with notes, logged actions, recorded votes.
3. **Trust that doesn’t depend on one person.** Records outlive committees.
4. **Everyone can ask.** Every resident can see, question and vote, in their own language, even by voice on Telegram.
5. **Less paperwork, more community.** Automation gives the Secretary their evenings back.
6. **A better country, one society at a time.** The belief layer. Use sparingly and sincerely.

### 7. Brand personality, voice and tone

**If SocietalPro were a person:** the respected elder neighbor who keeps the books open on the table, answers any question calmly, takes no sides and quietly makes everyone behave a little better.

| Trait | Means | Is NOT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Principled** | Stands for openness, clearly and calmly | Preachy or self-righteous |
| **Neutral** | Tools for everyone, no side-taking | Accusatory or political |
| **Grounded** | Practical, Indian, everyday | Abstract or ideological |
| **Respectful** | Dignity for committees and defaulters alike | Shaming or exposing |
| **Hopeful** | Believes small habits change a country | Cynical or outraged |

**Voice rule:** *Idealism in the belief, plainness in the product.* Short sentences, no slogans stacked on slogans, no outrage.

| Context | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manifesto / About | Sincere, conviction-led | “We learn honesty at home. Our society is the next classroom.” |
| Social (belief) | Thoughtful, warm | “Ask where the money goes. It’s a habit worth building.” |
| Social (product) | Clear, practical | “Every payment, one note. No more ‘what was this for?’” |
| Secretary content | Respectful, supportive | “Honest committees deserve proof. Here’s how to show yours.” |
| Payment reminders | Polite, private | “A friendly reminder: ₹X maintenance is due on [date].” |
| Voting notices | Neutral | “Voting is open on [issue] till [date]. One flat, one vote.” |
| Errors / support | Calm, accountable | “That was on our side. Here’s the fix.” |
| Telegram voice | Short, spoken-style | “Got it. Your request is logged and the Secretary can see it.” |

**Words we use:** open, on record, nothing hidden, together, ask, habit, proof, your society.

**Words we avoid:** corrupt (as a label for people), exposed, caught, expose, crackdown, revolution, anything naming individuals or parties.

### 8. Signature brand devices

- **Open Society badge:** a badge societies can display (“This society runs in the open”) once they meet set criteria (payments with notes, published reports, recorded votes). Gives you social proof and gently pressures nearby societies. *Define criteria carefully and make it about practices, never a moral rating.*
- **See. Ask. Decide.** The three-step habit loop as a recurring graphic and onboarding flow.
- **“Ask the right question” content series:** short posts and Reels (for example, “5 questions every member should ask at the AGM”).
- **The Open Ledger motif:** a visual of a ledger or window left open, used in illustrations.

### 9. Logo direction ✏️ *to be designed*

- **Concept (recommended):** a **house roofline with an open, see-through center**, like a window or door left open. It says *home → society → open*. Alternative: roofline with a check mark.
- **Wordmark:** “SocietalPro” in a rounded geometric sans, capital S and P only.
- **Voice/AI accent:** a small sound-wave line, used only in Telegram and voice graphics.
- **Versions:** horizontal, stacked, icon-only (avatar, favicon), one-color.
- **Rules:** clear space equal to the height of the “S”; minimum icon 24px and wordmark 120px; full color on Cloud or white; white on Ink Navy or Teal.
- **Avoid:** the national flag or tricolor, Ashoka Chakra, national emblem and government-style seals. Their use is legally restricted in India, and it would blur the line that this is a private product.

### 10. Color and type

Keeping the earlier palette. Its calm teal and navy read as trustworthy, not political.

| Role | Name | HEX | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | **Trust Teal** | `#0B7F70` | Logo, buttons, links |
| Dark | **Ink Navy** | `#0F1F3D` | Headings, text, dark backgrounds |
| CTA | **Signal Amber** | `#F59E0B` | Main CTA, “Open Society” badge highlights |
| Light | **Cloud** | `#F6F8FB` | Backgrounds |
| Soft | **Mist** | `#E3F4F1` | Cards, tags |
| Accent | **Aqua** | `#14B8A6` | Dark-background and voice accents |
| Text secondary | **Slate** | `#5B6678` | Captions |
| Status | **Green / Red** | `#16A34A` / `#DC2626` | Paid / overdue (soft wording) |

**Rules:** 60% Cloud/white, 25% navy and teal, 10% amber, 5% accents. Navy text on amber, not white. One amber CTA per section. Don’t pair saffron, white and green together (it evokes the flag).

**Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans (headings), Inter (body, tabular numerals for amounts), Noto Sans family for Indian languages. Sentence case. Manifesto and quote moments may use a serif (for example *Source Serif 4*) to add gravity.

### 11. Product truths the brand depends on

Your brand is now a promise of transparency, so the product has to back it. Before public launch, check:

- **Audit logs should be append-only,** with no edit or delete, even by admins. Only then can you say “every change is logged.”
- **Payment notes are mandatory** and visible to authorized members.
- **Role-based visibility** is clear (what residents see, what the committee sees, what only the Secretary sees).
- **Society-level reports** that residents can view (monthly income, expenses, balance).
- **Export and records** so a society can leave with its data, which proves you aren’t a lock-in black box.

If any of these aren’t built yet, soften the claim until they are.

### 14. Risk guardrails (since you’re taking a bold stance)

- **Stay private-sector and non-partisan.** No party, politician or government criticism in marketing. The “one day, the government too” idea lives in the manifesto as a hope about people’s habits, never as an attack. Phrase it as “ask the right questions”, not “hold them accountable”.
- **Never name or shame** committees, societies or individuals in content.
- **Customer stories need written consent** and should focus on process (“we started publishing monthly accounts”), not accusations.
- **Neutral tools:** don’t market features as ways to catch someone. Market them as ways to prove honesty.
- **Privacy:** visitor data is Secretary-only, and DPDP Act 2023 compliance needs a legal review (consent, purpose, retention).
- **AI voice:** state clearly that replies are AI-generated.
- **Payments:** “processed securely via Razorpay”, with no claims about holding funds.
- **Get a short legal review** of the manifesto and badge criteria before launch. It’s cheap insurance for a values-led brand.
