What are Monthly Text Credits?

Text credits power instant, reliable notifications sent to residents via WhatsApp and SMS. From maintenance bill generation and payment acknowledgments to emergency water-tank maintenance announcements, your society stays connected without anyone getting left behind.

What are Monthly Voice Credits?

Voice credits enable automated, multi-lingual AI calls for critical alerts — such as emergency AGM quorum calls, fire-drill notices, lift breakdown warnings, or urgent committee polls. It ensures elderly residents or non-smartphone users are informed instantly in their preferred language.
